# CaseSync Training Video Script
**Beatrice Loving Heart (BLH) — CaseSync Portal**
*Estimated runtime: ~12 minutes*

---

## 1. Intro [0:00 – 0:30]

**[VISUAL]** BLH logo fades in, transitions to the CaseSync login screen at blhcasesync.com.

**[NARRATION]**
"Welcome to CaseSync — the case management portal for Beatrice Loving Heart. CaseSync is where you manage clients, track every deadline, collaborate with your team, and stay on top of compliance. Whether you're a Supports Planner, Team Manager, or Supervisor, this video covers everything you need. Let's jump in."

---

## 2. Login [0:30 – 1:15]

**[VISUAL]** The login page. Cursor enters email and password. Dashboard loads with the premium greeting card visible.

**[NARRATION]**
"Go to blhcasesync.com and sign in with your email and password. If this is your first time, you'll have received an invitation email — click the link to set up your password. Make it strong: eight characters minimum, mixed case, with numbers or symbols.

Once you're in, you'll land on your Dashboard — your home base in CaseSync."

---

## 3. Dashboard Overview [1:15 – 3:30]

**[VISUAL]** Full dashboard view. Camera pans across greeting card, urgency pills, suggested focus, stat cards, week strip, alert banner, client grid.

**[NARRATION]**
"Your Dashboard starts with a personalized greeting showing your name, today's date, and urgency status pills. These colored pills tell you immediately how many items are overdue, due this week, and need contact. Click any pill to filter your view instantly.

Below the greeting, the Suggested Focus card tells you the smartest thing to do next. It might say 'Start with overdue pressure' or 'Log a contact — no outreach in 7+ days.'

Next, four premium stat cards show your key numbers — Active Clients, Overdue, Due This Week, and No Contact 7+ Days. Each card has an animated arc showing the ratio. Click any card to filter your client list.

The Week Strip below shows 7 days of upcoming deadlines. Days are color-coded: red for heavy overdue, amber for 2+ deadlines, olive for 1. Click a day to filter.

The Alert Banner highlights urgency counts as clickable pills. And below everything, your client cards — each one showing the client's name, urgency status, health score ring, risk badges, and deadline pills."

---

## 4. Client Cards [3:30 – 4:30]

**[VISUAL]** Hover over a client card — it lifts with a colored glow. Show the urgency date pills, health ring, and risk badge. Show swipe gestures on mobile.

**[NARRATION]**
"Each client card is packed with information at a glance. The colored left border tells you the worst urgency — red means something is overdue. The health score ring in the top corner shows overall goal progress.

Inline date pills only show non-green dates — the ones that need attention. Risk badges say 'HIGH' or 'MED' right next to the name. The footer shows days since last contact, goal percentage, and a quick Log Contact button.

Hover any card to see it lift with an urgency-colored glow. On mobile, swipe right to quick-log a contact, or swipe left to pin the client."

---

## 5. Client Detail Page [4:30 – 6:30]

**[VISUAL]** Click a client card. The 2-column detail page opens. Show the hero header, date tile grid, hover a tile to show the popup, scroll the sidebar.

**[NARRATION]**
"Click any client name to open their full detail page. This is the pride of CaseSync.

At the top, the hero header shows the client's name large and bold, with their ID, category, eligibility code, and assigned planner. The health score ring sits prominently in the top right. Below the name, status pills show how many items are overdue and when you last made contact.

The page uses a two-column layout. On the left, you'll see the Key Deadlines grid — ten interactive date tiles covering every critical deadline.

Here's the magic: hover any date tile and a popup appears showing the urgency tier, a large countdown — like '12 days overdue' or '3 days left' — and an action prompt for overdue items. Each tile also shows a colored countdown chip so you can scan urgency without hovering.

Below the deadline grid, you'll find Contact details, Plans and Assessments, CO Details, Notes, and the Activity Log — all in collapsible sections.

On the right sidebar: AI Intelligence where you can ask BLH Bot about this client or generate an AI summary, Client Info with reassignment controls, and collapsible sections for Med Tech, Forms, Authorizations, and Reporting.

Click Edit in the hero header to update any field. Changes are tracked in the Activity Log automatically."

---

## 6. Bulk Contact Logger [6:30 – 7:15]

**[VISUAL]** On the dashboard, click Select. Check several clients. Click "📞 Log 5 Contacts". Show the modal with date picker, type dropdown, and note field. Click submit — clients update instantly.

**[NARRATION]**
"When you need to log contact for multiple clients at once — say after a day of phone calls — use the Bulk Contact Logger.

Click Select in the toolbar, check the clients you contacted, then click the Log Contacts button. Set the date, choose the contact type, add an optional note, and submit. All selected clients update instantly."

---

## 7. Calendar [7:15 – 8:00]

**[VISUAL]** Navigate to Calendar. Show month view with urgency-colored cells. Hover a day to show the tooltip popup with client details. Switch to week view.

**[NARRATION]**
"The Calendar gives you a visual overview of all deadlines. Day cells are color-tinted by urgency — red-tinted days have overdue items, amber for due soon.

Hover any day with events to see a popup showing every client and their deadlines for that day, color-coded by urgency. Click any event to jump to the client profile.

Use Month view for planning ahead and Week view for daily scheduling."

---

## 8. BLH Bot & AI [8:00 – 8:45]

**[VISUAL]** Click the purple FAB button. BLH Bot opens. Type "What's due this week?" and get a response. Then on a client detail page, show the AI Intelligence card — ask a question and generate a summary.

**[NARRATION]**
"BLH Bot is your AI assistant. Click the purple button on any page and ask anything — 'What's due this week?', 'Which clients need contact?', 'What does eligibility code CLS mean?'

On any client's detail page, the AI Intelligence card in the sidebar lets you ask client-specific questions or generate a one-click AI summary of their current status and outstanding items."

---

## 9. Team Manager & Supervisor Features [8:45 – 10:15]

**[VISUAL]** Show the Supervisor Control Panel — org health meter, urgent client list, stat cards, planner workload cards with animated load bars and metric chips. Hover a metric chip to show tooltip. Click to the Team page. Show the Admin panel.

**[NARRATION]**
"Team Managers and Supervisors have additional tools.

Team Managers see a Team page with planner workload cards. Each planner shows an animated load bar, color-coded by overdue severity, with metric chips you can hover for details. Click any planner to see their clients.

Supervisors land on the Control Panel — an executive overview with an Org Health meter, a live list of urgent clients, and org-wide stat cards. The planner workload section shows every planner across the org with their overdue, due, and quiet counts.

Supervisors also have the Admin Panel for managing users — inviting new staff, changing roles, and deactivating accounts. And the Audit Log records every action in CaseSync for HIPAA compliance."

---

## 10. Mobile App [10:15 – 10:45]

**[VISUAL]** Show CaseSync on a phone. Show the bottom navigation bar, swipe gestures on cards, and the full-screen PWA experience.

**[NARRATION]**
"CaseSync works beautifully on your phone. On iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Chrome's Install App option.

The mobile layout has a bottom navigation bar for one-thumb access. Client cards support swipe gestures — right to log contact, left to pin. Every feature works on mobile."

---

## 11. Outro [10:45 – 11:15]

**[VISUAL]** Return to dashboard. BLH logo fades in.

**[NARRATION]**
"That's CaseSync. You now know how to navigate your dashboard, manage client profiles with the interactive detail page, log contacts in bulk, use the calendar, and get help from BLH Bot.

If you ever have a question, start with BLH Bot — just click the purple button. For anything that needs a human answer, reach out to your Team Manager or Supervisor.

Welcome to CaseSync. Let's do great work for the people we serve."
